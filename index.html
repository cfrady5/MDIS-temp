About the Summit

The Midwest Defense Innovation Summit advances national security through mission-driven technology and capability development.

Powered by the Applied Research Institute, MDIS convenes senior defense leaders, policymakers, industry, academia, and regional partners to examine how emerging technologies are tested, transitioned, and fielded at speed in support of national defense priorities.
